## New

- **People who write to you join the address book.** Every new letter to one of your identities and every new incoming chat adds its sender to Contacts, under a generated name. Your own identities, chans and people you removed from the book before are skipped. Mail that arrived while the app was closed counts too. Old senders are not added in bulk, so contacts you removed stay removed; a chat from before this version needs one click on "+".
- **Ask for a nickname.** A "?" beside a contact's address in the chat header and beside From and To in the reader asks the default nickbot for the contact's nickname. The answer takes minutes and is kept with the contact. A "+" beside the address of a stranger adds them.
- **Check nicknames.** The Identities page has a new button, an @ icon beside Join a channel… and New identity. It asks the default nickbot for the nicknames of all your identities and fills the Nickname column, so a second computer that has only your keys sees the nicknames you already registered. Nothing is asked until you press it.
- **Flatpak.** ynotbit is packaged for Flathub as `io.github.yshurik.ynotbit`.

## Network

- **One nickname per address.** The nickbot keeps one nickname for an address, of any sigil, and a name an address gives up is free at once for anyone. A registry in which an address already has two names must be reduced to one before the new nickbot starts.
